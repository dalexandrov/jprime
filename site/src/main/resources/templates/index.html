The Conference

jPrime is a conference with talks on Java, various languages on the JVM, mobile, web and best practices. It's run by the Bulgarian Java User Group and backed by the biggest companies in the country.

jPrime features a combination of great international speakers along with the best presenters from Bulgaria and the Balkans. It is divided in two tracks and provides great opportunities for learning, hacking, networking and fun.

Why Attend

The community driven Java conference in Bulgaria! Come and become part of the community.

Meet Speakers
One of the main goals to go to a conference is not only to learn something new from the sessions, but also to speak with the speakers. At jPrime there is no speaker room, the speakers are outside with all of you, so you can speak with them freely.
Tech Sessions
All sessions are selected via CFP process and voting, there are no sponsor sessions, there are 0 marketing and HR talks. This is not a bla bla conference, it's a practical and awesome conference!
Workshops
Since 2017 we also have workshops. Just bring your laptop - there is no extra cost and no pre-workshop signing, just visit the workshop and participate in the training.
Free Swags
We always have a bag of goodies - not only sponsor goodies but also jPrime goodies like shirts, notebooks, flash drives, stickers, etc.
Networking
Main part of the conference is the networking, speaking with each other, and sharing experience and knowledge and ... well just have fun.
Tech Insights
Get additional insights from the community, the speakers and the sponsors during our lunch breaks, coffee breaks .. or beer breaks ;)
