Transport

Conference hall address: Tsarigradsko Shosse 111B, Sofia, Bulgaria

Sofia Tech Park is located 6 km away from the Sofia International Airport. It usually takes up to 10 min to reach the venue with a car. Average taxi price 0,40/0,45 EUR per km.

For the conference guests, we recommend Vega Hotel Sofia which is located around a 20-minute walk from the venue.
